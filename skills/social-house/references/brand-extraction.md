# Brand extraction from supplied assets

Use when a logo, product card, packaging, endcard, profile grid or prior post is supplied. Do this automatically; do not make a small business owner fill a brand questionnaire before a first draft.

## Build a provisional Brand Card

Record only what source assets visibly support:

- **Palette:** 2–4 approximate functional colours: base, contrast, accent, neutral.
- **Type character:** serif/sans/display, geometric/humanist/industrial, dense/open. Do not claim a typeface name from a raster logo.
- **Logo treatment:** permitted crop, clear space, contrast needs, white-background lockup or removable background, endcard use.
- **Visual temperament:** for example quiet/technical, warm/artisanal, sharp/contemporary. Mark as inferred unless brand materials state it.
- **Signature candidates:** a repeated crop, material close-up, framing or transition that can recur subtly.

The card is a production constraint, not a justification for copying the logo into every frame.

## Do not infer these

Never infer exact fonts, brand values, product composition, price, availability, target market, garment fit, heritage claim, customer promise or permission to alter a logo. Ask only if one of these blocks the current deliverable.

## Use during production

- Choose the temporary font system by type character, then verify it at phone size. Keep max two families.
- Pull colour from real brand asset only for type, rules, endcard transitions and minor graphic detail. Do not tint real product proof to force palette harmony.
- Keep the logo out of hero proof unless it is the intentional endcard or cover lockup.
- If the white field is part of a supplied logo/endcard, preserve it unless background removal can be made cleanly and is authorised.

Mark every Brand Card field as `confirmed`, `visible evidence` or `inferred`. It becomes an editable Brand Memory after user correction.
