# Input contract reference

## Minimum viable brief

Accept any format, then extract:

- real assets and their likely subject;
- what is being sold;
- target buyer/region/language;
- desired action;
- facts that are explicitly verified;
- facts/visuals that must not be guessed;
- brand/reference links and rights status if supplied.

If no action is given, suggest the most plausible one and mark it as an assumption. If no real source exists, ask for a real source before making a product-proof post.

## Smallest useful reshoot requests

- Texture/material: `one 3–5 second macro clip moving through natural side light`.
- Product shape/fit: `one vertical full product/use shot with 2 seconds of stillness`.
- Colour/range: `one evenly lit top-down image of the complete colour range`.
- Process: `one continuous 5-second clip of the meaningful hand/machine action`.
- Human scale: `one clip showing product beside/in use by a person or familiar object`.

## Asset labels

- `real proof`: supports a product claim.
- `real supporting`: genuine environment, process or person, but not decisive claim proof.
- `generated context`: aesthetic support; cannot state/depict product fact.
- `graphic`: type, logo, diagrams, subtitle treatment.
