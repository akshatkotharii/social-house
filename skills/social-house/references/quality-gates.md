# Quality gates reference

## Product truth

- Every claim is a verified fact or visibly shown in a real proof asset.
- Product-specific colour, motif, material, scale and performance are never generated or guessed.
- Generated frames are labelled and do not carry decisive proof.

## Brand and human feel

- The creative has one specific remembered moment.
- It fits the brand feel, voice, type system and visual signature.
- It could not work for unrelated brands with only a logo swap.
- It proves rather than announces quality.

## Layout and accessibility

- Phone-size legibility is checked.
- Type stays out of product proof and platform UI zones.
- Reel works with sound on and sound off.
- Captions are timed, readable and deliberate.
- Carousel is checked slide-by-slide and as a grid.

## Conversion

- One primary outcome is explicit.
- CTA matches the business’s actual response route.
- Caption adds value beyond the on-frame copy.
- A person can respond to the CTA immediately.

## Trend, rights and publishing

- Live trend/sound claims include source/date/context; otherwise use a search query and alternative.
- Asset and music rights are known or declared unknown.
- No post/ad/message is sent without explicit authorisation.

## Status decision

- `publish-ready`: every applicable gate passes.
- `creative-ready, needs proof`: creative is sound, but a specified real asset/fact is missing.
- `exploration only`: aesthetic concept cannot responsibly represent the actual product.
