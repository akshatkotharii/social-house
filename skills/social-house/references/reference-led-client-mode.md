# Reference-led client mode

Use when a client supplies a Reel, carousel, account, screenshot or saved post and asks for content “like this.” Goal: upload minimal real product media plus reference and get an original, brand-fitting asset with little back-and-forth.

## Intake: what client supplies

Accept a messy message. Minimum useful inputs: real product image/video, what is sold, target buyer and desired action. Reference link or upload is optional. Use saved Brand Memory for logo, voice, fonts, endcard, rights, market and previous content. Ask one compact question only if material truth, rights, buyer or conversion path is missing.

## Reference translation

1. Confirm reference is viewable. If blocked, ask for a screen recording or screenshots; do not pretend to inspect it.
2. Extract mechanics: first-frame question, shot/proof sequence, hold and cut rhythm, framing/movement, type roles, highlight logic, sound arc, cover, caption opening and CTA. Separate observations from inferred intent/performance.
3. Identify what client likes and what should not transfer. Write one sentence: “Keep [communication mechanism]; replace [creator-specific expression] with [client's real product proof and brand language].”
4. Compare mechanism with source-material ceiling. If reference requires a model, shop, process, testimonial or exact colour absent from client assets, request one high-value real shot or choose a narrower version. Generated context may support mood but cannot imply merchandise proof.
5. Make an original format card: objective, remembered moment, real proof, shot progression, text/audio grammar, cover and failure signs. Do not copy distinctive sequence, wording, footage, voice, branding or licensed audio.

## Trend and publishing research

When live access exists, search recent target-market and category references through authorised platform/account routes. Record retrieval date, region, creator, observed format, sound identifier, audience fit and commercial-use status. Cross-check with the client's own account insights when authorised. “Trending” needs dated evidence; audio unavailable for business use or paid boosting cannot be silently placed in the export. Where native sound rights are uncertain, deliver an edit with licensed fallback and a clear in-app audio-selection instruction.

Hashtags and captions are not a substitute for creative quality. Use a few relevant search terms and buyer vocabulary; do not promise reach from a tag set or copy a competitor's caption. If regional search or account insights are unavailable, say so and proceed with a truthful, non-trend concept.

## Delivery contract

Internally choose generation, editing, voice, type and audio tools. Client should not have to coordinate them. Deliver one recommended Reel or carousel, cover, post caption, alt text, CTA/DM reply, real-versus-generated map, rights/trend note and honest QA status. Keep posting/boosting approval separate from asset creation. If a missing shot prevents a strong result, hand back one exact filming instruction, not a weak “publish-ready” imitation.

## Prototype test

Test with one real client brief and one reference. Compare output with reference on *mechanics* and with product on truth; obtain buyer/client judgement on attractiveness, comprehension, trust and DM intent. Treat “same thing” as a user-friendly input request, not a promise to clone or a proof of performance.
