# Knowledge ingestion workflow

Use this whenever user supplies a reference, research article, expert feedback, tool, account, performance result or critique of generated output.

## Required outcome

Do not stop at summary or storage. Determine what new input should change in Social House, apply that change at correct layer, and record why.

## 1. Classify input

- `creative reference`: Reel, post, carousel, campaign, account or design example.
- `expert practice`: rule or workflow from practitioner.
- `research claim`: platform, audience, algorithm, marketing or production claim.
- `tool candidate`: generation, editing, voice, analytics, research or publishing tool.
- `performance evidence`: account insights, experiment, DMs, sales or lead quality.
- `failure evidence`: weak output, wrong product detail, poor layout, generic copy, unnatural voice or broken workflow.

One input may have multiple classes.

## 2. Extract decision-bearing evidence

Capture only information that may change work:

- source and collection date;
- market, platform, sector and intended audience;
- visible/verified observation versus author claim;
- creative mechanics: hook, proof, crop, pacing, visual world, sound, voice, text, cover, CTA;
- evidence strength and known conflicts;
- relevance to Social House;
- copyright, licence, access, pricing or provider risk.

Do not treat likes/views alone as causal proof. Do not treat a Reddit claim, creator opinion or vendor page as platform fact.

## 3. Choose disposition

Every input receives one:

- `adopt`: strong enough to change current behaviour.
- `test`: useful hypothesis; add controlled evaluation before making rule.
- `sector-only`: useful for one category, not universal.
- `tool-route`: add as optional capability with trigger, fallback and cost/access notes.
- `reference-only`: mechanics worth retaining, no rule change yet.
- `reject`: misleading, copied, unverifiable, redundant or harmful.

State disposition briefly. Silence is not disposition.

## 4. Apply at correct layer

- Change `SKILL.md` only for cross-format decisions needed in most runs.
- Put conditional workflows in `references/`.
- Put public rationale and longer research in repository `docs/` or `research/`.
- Add or update a behavioural test when input came from failure or changes a critical rule.
- Add tool only after checking exact capability, input/output, access, cost, rights, failure mode and fallback. Tool popularity is not enough.
- Update Brand Memory or sector knowledge when learning belongs to one client/category.

Prefer changing an existing rule over adding a duplicate. Remove superseded guidance.

## 5. Prove application

For each ingestion, report:

- what was learned;
- disposition;
- exact skill/doc/test changed;
- what future output will do differently;
- remaining uncertainty or proposed test.

If nothing should change, record rejection reason. “Saved for later” alone is failure.

## Rule promotion standard

Promote to universal rule only when at least one applies:

- direct platform/primary-source requirement;
- repeated expert agreement plus compatible observed examples;
- repeated success across relevant client outputs;
- clear prevention of a demonstrated failure without harming other sectors.

Otherwise keep as hypothesis, reference mechanic or sector-specific guidance.

## Updating trends

Trend data expires. Record date, region/account context, commercial-use status and source. A trend should affect current concept choice, not become permanent skill doctrine. Retire stale trend entries instead of accumulating them.
