# Text, motion and audio gate

Read before any Reel that uses on-frame copy, subtitles, narration, music or SFX. Full design rationale lives in [`docs/TEXT-AND-AUDIO-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`](../../../docs/TEXT-AND-AUDIO-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md).

## Minimum artefacts before assembly

1. **Source ceiling decision:** supplied proof can support the premise, or one exact reshoot request is issued.
2. **Text map:** for every line, shot range, buyer purpose, protected proof region, stable safe area, background contrast, role and entry/exit event.
3. **Audio ledger:** for every track, source/rights, role, time range, level, fades and intended cut/reveal cue.
4. **Sound choice:** voice/no voice, music/original/natural sound, language and performance direction, trend check status, rights route and clean-master fallback.
5. **Voice-to-picture map:** spoken phrase and timing paired with proof shot, on-screen copy, emotional turn, movement/cut and sound cue.

## Rules

- No text is valid when product proof carries the moment better.
- Every line answers `what`, `why`, or `what next` for the current buyer moment.
- No generic luxury language, default translucent boxes or inferred “premium” font pairings.
- Check text against every frame of its hold at phone size. If safe region is unstable, move/omit text or redesign shot.
- Motion responds to cut, hand action, reveal, colour shift, voice phrase or soundtrack moment. No automatic bounce/typewriter/glitch/karaoke styling.
- Keep type families to brand system. Use temporary pairing only as a documented, tested fallback.
- Use only deliberate audio layers. Voice/natural sound/music/SFX/endcard need planned handoffs; no accidental overlap.
- A supplied endcard is a separate final beat, not background branding. Let voice-led CTA finish first; trim its visual/audio to the shortest readable contact hold, fade its audio in/out, and verify it does not cut off a spoken final word.
- When a Reel uses or recommends Instagram trend audio, check current target-region evidence near production and verify account availability and commercial use separately. Agent Reach account/post discovery alone cannot establish audio rank or licence. If the current Instagram audio surface is inaccessible, report that, provide a search path or ask for a user-selected track, and make a clean master with a rights-safe fallback.
- Choose voice language, delivery and sound against the buyer and story. Ask once when a missing language, speaker or paid-use decision materially changes the result; otherwise label the audience-based default.
- In a voice-led Reel, each phrase must describe, deepen or sell the proof visible in that interval. Align breaths, emphasis and pauses with reveals or actions; move the line or shot when they do not match.

## Editorial context paired with product proof

When supplied product media is strong enough to prove texture or colour but weak at showing use-context, generated editorial frames may create desire without impersonating the product. Use a two-tier asset map:

- **Editorial context:** a model, setting or silhouette. It may establish the intended wearer or category, but must not assert an exact shade, weave, fit or availability.
- **Product proof:** real supplied media immediately verifies the material detail, colour range or other product claim that matters to the buyer.

Alternate context and proof only when that contrast advances the story. A good short narrated wholesale pattern is: 1.5–2 seconds of context, 1.5–2 seconds of macro proof, 2–2.5 seconds of second context, 2–2.5 seconds of range proof, a short final detail, then a 3–3.5 second action. Treat those durations as a tested option, not a template. Never repeat a swatch merely to fill time.

For this pattern, use slow, restrained camera movement on a still context frame and hard or visually matched cuts into proof. Do not add light leaks, glitch, blur or a transition card just to make it feel “cool.” The effect must clarify a material reveal or scene handoff. If no added music has rights, emotional fit and a clean handoff, a voice-led master is stronger than an unrelated music bed.

## Release blocker

Inspect entire render with sound on and sound off, then key frames from each text interval. If audio cannot be heard, status is `creative-ready, audio unverified`, never `publish-ready`.
