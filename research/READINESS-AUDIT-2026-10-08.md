# Social House readiness audit — 8 October 2026

## Verdict

Social House has a useful editorial specification and a credible product-truth policy. It is not yet a reliable one-photo-to-publish-ready Reel system. The repository contains Markdown guidance and behavioural cases, but no reusable intake, brand-memory, asset-ledger, rendering or QA runner. `social-house` is not installed in the current Codex skill directories. Prior Nakoda output was rated 3/10 by the user; no later benchmark demonstrates that the new rules prevent those failures.

The most important expert feedback is preserved verbatim in `skills/social-house/references/expert-feedback.md` and now routed from `SKILL.md`. The curated textile reference analysis is also inside the portable skill. These additions fix a knowledge access failure, not the production gap.

## What is strong

- Real product media is authoritative; synthetic colour, texture, garment and performance claims are blocked.
- Expert direction covers brand feel, tactile proof, covers, grids, typography, negative space, movement, sound, human copy and conversion.
- Four supplied textile Reels have observation-level mechanics analyses. They offer more useful direction than generic hook formulas.
- DM outcome is specific. The workflow distinguishes qualified enquiries from reach.
- Creative release requires full render inspection; technical export alone is insufficient.

## Where the current system overpromises

1. **One photo cannot show touch or drape.** A crop or parallax effect can preserve the weave, but it cannot invent hands, movement, weight or accurate garment behaviour. A one-photo Reel can be a strong teaser or shade reveal. A convincing tactile sales Reel usually needs one 3–6 second real handling clip and a clean range photo, or an existing product clip library.
2. **The skill is guidance, not an automated content house.** There is no implemented saved Brand Memory, reference retrieval, provider connector, storyboard-to-render template, human voice selection, cover generator or visual/audio QA harness in this repository.
3. **Trend research is not live.** Agent Reach doctor on 8 October 2026 found Instagram and Facebook OpenCLI extension present but disconnected. Current sound, reel trend and account-level fit cannot be verified from that route. User-supplied links have partial analysis; carousel references lack slide-level inspection.
4. **No generator is proven best for fabric.** Google Flow is a candidate, but its help page says content generated in India receives a visible watermark. Its cost and fidelity must be checked in the user's account. Runway's free tier has one-time credits and watermarked video. Generated cloth motion and model garments remain prone to visual drift.
5. **A publishing pack is not a sales system without operations.** Brand account, shade card, actual stock/MOQ/price process, response owner and lead tracking must be set once per business. Two lines per product are feasible only after that onboarding.
6. **Rules can create generic output.** A standard 15-second ladder, fixed slide sequence, three-concept quota, constant CTA/endcard and mandatory voice can turn taste into a template. The skill must choose among sale, desire, craft, education and community jobs based on buyer and assets, then vary rhythm and presentation.
7. **No independent outcome test exists.** There is no controlled comparison of outputs, merchant review, buyer response or qualified DM lift. Claims about follower growth or conversion would be speculative.

## Platform evidence, translated carefully

- Meta says Instagram and Facebook use multiple ranking systems, not one universal algorithm. Feed ranking predicts actions such as likes, comments, saves and profile taps; surfaces differ. Source: [Meta system cards overview](https://ai.meta.com/blog/how-ai-powers-experiences-facebook-instagram-system-cards/), [Instagram Feed card](https://ai.meta.com/tools/system-cards/instagram-feed-ranking/).
- Meta's January 2026 engineering summary of a 2025 RecSys paper says watch time and likes alone are noisy; its newer Facebook Reels model adds survey-based interest fit, including audio, style, mood and motivation. The full ACM paper was inaccessible during this review, so this claim rests on Meta's own summary. For Social House this supports buyer relevance and honest product interest, not a precise hook-length formula. Source: [Meta Engineering](https://engineering.fb.com/2026/01/14/ml-applications/adapting-the-facebook-reels-recsys-ai-model-based-on-user-feedback/).
- Meta reported greater recommendation of original Instagram posts in the US in late 2025. This is a platform result, not a promise for Indian wholesale accounts. Source: [Meta 2026 update](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/01/2026-ai-drives-performance/).
- For paid Reels, Meta recommends vertical creative, usable audio and protected safe zones; its published test outcomes span mixed advertisers and cannot be assumed for textile wholesale. Source: [Meta Reels ads guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads).
- This review found no public platform paper establishing an optimal Instagram carousel slide count, universal posting time, or guaranteed boost threshold. Design for useful swipes and test against the business's own qualified enquiries.

## Next proof of readiness

Use one real textile SKU with a two-line brief and its available media. Produce three distinct outputs: a proof-led Reel, a restrained editorial Reel, and a useful buyer carousel. Ask 3–5 target buyers or textile operators what product they think they saw, what fact they trust, and whether they would DM; get a designer/editor to inspect crop, type, sound and pacing. Record failures before revising the skill. Compare against the existing 3/10 Nakoda output. This tests actual media, not just Markdown compliance.

The smallest practical capture kit: one clean full-range photo, one macro still, one 3–6 second vertical hand/drape clip, and one verified line about the buyer use case. For a returning brand, save brand feel, voice pronunciation, logo/type, buyer geography, shade-card reply and forbidden claims once. Only then should the repeated SKU brief shrink to 2–3 lines.
