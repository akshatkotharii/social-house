# Source-material ceiling

Social House must diagnose what supplied media can honestly communicate before it selects a concept, writes copy or chooses a generator. Minimal input should reduce friction, not lower the truth standard.

## Principle

Make the strongest asset that supplied material can support. Do not stretch a swatch, catalogue photo or one pan into a fabricated product campaign.

## Evidence classes

| Supplied proof | It can support | It cannot support without further proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Swatch or colour card | colour range, visible weave/motif, labelled material only when user confirms it | finished garment fit, true drape, comfort, stock, price, blend percentage, styling result |
| Hand-held fabric clip | texture, fold, real handling, colour transition, human scale | a full collection story, tailoring result, factory capacity or wear performance |
| Finished garment photo/video | visible cut, styling, fit on that wearer, details shown | size range, universal fit, unshown colourways or material performance |
| Process clip | actual craft/action/process step | final product claim unless it appears or is explicitly verified |
| Customer proof | that person's approved experience | a universal result or performance guarantee |

## Decision outcomes

1. **Enough proof:** make one narrowly scoped asset with an honest goal.
2. **Creative-ready, needs proof:** choose concept and package, then ask for one 3–5 second shot or one fact that unlocks the missing buyer question.
3. **Exploration only:** use generated/editorial visuals to plan a shoot, but do not represent the product as final merchandise.

## Minimum reshoot requests

Ask for only one requested shot at a time. Explain why it changes the result.

| Missing story | Exact request |
| --- | --- |
| Fabric drape/hand feel | “Film 5 seconds vertically in daylight. Lift one fabric edge once, keep camera still, include natural sound.” |
| Shade choice | “Lay complete shade range evenly under neutral light. Hold 2 seconds before moving.” |
| Shirt/trouser use | “Film one real stitched sample front, side and a close fabric detail; no filter.” |
| Craft/process credibility | “Film one uninterrupted 5-second action: cutting, stitching, weaving or finishing, with hands and machine/tool visible.” |
| Human scale | “Show product beside a familiar object or on an approved person for 3–5 seconds.” |

## Generator boundary

AI can extend a visual world, create an editorial environment or add non-product motion. It cannot fill missing merchandise evidence. If a generated model, shirt or textile is used, label it internally as `generated context`, place real proof near it, and do not write copy implying the generated garment is supplied inventory.

## Example: one stripe-swatch video

A swatch pan supports a focused range Reel: moving weave, colour family, product name, verified use and catalogue/DM CTA. It does not support “see how the shirt falls,” styling claims, full-shop experience or a garment campaign. Request a real stitched sample or controlled drape shot before making those claims.
