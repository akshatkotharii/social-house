# Reference library protocol

References are a design-research library, not a copy queue. Store a link, screenshot/asset only when permitted, collector note, collection date, account/creator, sector, format, objective, and a mechanics analysis.

## Mechanics record

For every promoted reference, record:

- first-frame hook and whether it is visual, spoken, written, or sonic;
- product proof: what is actually demonstrated rather than claimed;
- shot/crop progression and visual world;
- pace, motion continuity, transition logic and holds;
- typography, subtitle and cover behaviour;
- sound/voice role and commercial-use caveat;
- caption/CTA path;
- what makes it feel human, distinctive or premium;
- what should **not** be copied;
- a translation hypothesis for the target brand.

## Promotion rules

A reference becomes a reusable pattern only after it has an original translation recipe. Examples:

- “Open a textile Reel with 0.6–1.2 seconds of real macro weave under a moving band of side light; keep copy off that frame.”
- “For a premium carousel, create slide rhythm by alternating close proof, quiet space, full context and information—not by making every slide a text poster.”
- “A trend must pass freshness, commercial eligibility, audience relevance, brand fit and product-truth checks before the system offers it.”

## Anti-copy rule

Never lift a creator’s complete sequence, distinctive visuals, voiceover, caption, audio, edit, or individual asset. Translate the underlying communication mechanism into the target brand’s actual product, visual world, facts and audience need.
