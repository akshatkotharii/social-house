# Research and reference notes

## Evidence standard

Social content advice is full of confident anecdotes. Social House separates:

- **Direct observation:** a supplied reference’s visible hook, cropping, pacing, cover or CTA.
- **Expert operating feedback:** the detailed practitioner feedback gathered in the working project.
- **Community hypothesis:** an observation from practitioners that should be tested against an individual account, not treated as causal law.
- **Platform-dependent fact:** must be checked at the time of use because rights, sound availability, features and delivery conditions change.

## Existing skill analysis: charlie947/social-media-skills

The public [social-media-skills repository](https://github.com/charlie947/social-media-skills) is a useful set of discrete social tools: voice building, research, post scoring, analytics, Reels scripting, and carousel creation.

Useful ideas to adopt:

- build and persist a brand voice rather than prompting from scratch;
- analyse reference mechanics before writing;
- use slide-by-slide carousel blueprints;
- identify missing capabilities and configuration plainly;
- include a review step rather than treating a text prompt as a finished social asset.

What does not meet this project’s need by itself:

- its [Reels scripting workflow](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/charlie947/social-media-skills/main/skills/reels-scripting/SKILL.md) is oriented around a talking-head/newsletter format and produces scripts, not product-proof video packages;
- its [carousel workflow](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/charlie947/social-media-skills/main/skills/gemini-carousel/SKILL.md) produces image prompts and is centred on a LinkedIn-style format rather than photo-led, feed-tested product carousels;
- neither is a closed-loop product truth, rendering, visual QA, trend-rights, DM conversion and performance-learning system.

Social House should borrow the capability gates and structured briefs—not their fixed content formula.

## Community observations to test, not declare as law

Recent discussions in [r/smallbusiness](https://www.reddit.com/r/smallbusiness/comments/1szehzp/how_do_you_handle_social_media_for_your_small/) and [r/InstagramMarketing](https://www.reddit.com/r/InstagramMarketing/comments/1ou74f8/im_a_professional_social_media_growth_strategist/) repeatedly favour authentic product/process footage, natural light, useful storytelling, repurposing real footage across formats, and fast engagement with the earliest audience. These are practitioner observations, not controlled evidence. Use them as hypotheses in the measurement loop.

## Current reference collection

The project’s private source collection includes fabric/editorial references and the learned mechanics: tactile macro openings, real process worlds, premium carousel rhythm, trend adaptation, natural material proof, and no-text-over-product restraint. The reference collector must keep original URL, platform, creator/account, collection date, observed mechanics, sector, and usage note.

Never treat a reference as a license to reuse its video, audio, copy, or distinctive creative sequence.

## Tools under consideration

- **ElevenLabs:** optional voiceover provider; suitability depends on account rights, voice selection, language quality and cost.
- **Gemini/Kling/Runway/other image-to-video tools:** optional support-context generation. They cannot verify or accurately recreate an unseen product.
- **ChatCut / Hyperframes:** production and editing surfaces for video assembly; the product needs a final visual/audio QA pass independent of the renderer.
- **StringTune:** a browser-motion runtime useful for a web companion/interactive launch experience, not an MP4 Reel renderer. See the [StringTune site](https://string-tune.fiddle.digital/).

The baseline must remain free-first and provider-optional. “No cost” cannot honestly mean every generated clip, voice, stock licence, live data source, or API will always be free.
