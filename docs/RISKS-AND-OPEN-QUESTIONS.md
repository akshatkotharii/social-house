# Risks and open questions

## Non-negotiable realities

1. **A single photo is not a content shoot.** A good creative direction can be made from it, but it cannot honestly prove movement, fit, scale, texture, construction or a broad product range. The system needs a minimum-proof threshold and a one-line reshoot request.
2. **Generated visuals are a trust risk for products.** They can improve mood but can alter colour, material, detail, scale and how something behaves. They must be segregated from real product proof.
3. **Trend and audio access is unstable.** Availability changes by geography, account type, commercial eligibility, platform and day. The system should produce a dated, editable sound search card—not falsely guarantee a “trending sound.”
4. **There is no universal best time, format or boost threshold.** Account-specific baselines and actual qualified outcomes matter more than generic social advice.
5. **Content cannot repair a weak sales operation.** If a business cannot answer DMs, provide catalogue/price details, fulfil reliably or route leads, content will expose the gap rather than solve it.
6. **Autoposting and advertising require consent and permissions.** They remain separate explicit actions, never silent behaviour of a content skill.
7. **One-go output still needs internal review.** This must be designed as invisible expert passes with a truthful stop condition, not as “the user will iterate until it works.”

## Questions for the first product decision

1. Should v0 be a **Codex skill for an operator** (more flexible, easier to test) or a **guided product interface for founders** (less friction, more engineering)? The proposed order is operator skill first, then turn repeated decisions into a guided UI.
2. For the first sector tests, is the primary business outcome **B2B wholesale DMs**, **retail sales**, **store visits**, or **catalogue/sample requests**? This changes the content jobs, CTA, and lead tracker.
3. Do the two textile companies have a repeatable asset capture routine? If not, we should ship a one-page mobile shoot checklist alongside the skill before promising one-go video.
4. Which output should be true v0: a fully rendered Reel first, a carousel first, or both with a deliberately limited set of content spines?
5. What is the acceptable boundary for AI support imagery in a product post? My recommendation: real product must be shown for every decisive product claim; generated imagery can only establish abstract context or clearly labelled styling concepts.
6. What data sources can users legally and practically connect in v0—manual exports, Meta insights, an Instagram URL, a reference folder, or only assets they upload? This decides the trend/analytics architecture.
7. Is the repository intended to be public open source now? If yes, choose a licence and remove any client-specific media, account URLs, passwords and private metrics before pushing.

## Proposed v0 scope

- One real-asset content pack.
- One Reel or one carousel, not an entire month’s calendar.
- Brand Memory stored in editable markdown/YAML.
- Reference analysis from user-supplied links/images.
- Manual or authorised post-performance import.
- Internal gates with `publish-ready`, `creative-ready, needs proof`, or `exploration only` status.
- No autonomous posting, paid spend, unverified trend claims, or fake product visualisation.

This is intentionally narrower than “AI social agency.” It gives us a chance to prove quality and conversion before automating higher-risk actions.
