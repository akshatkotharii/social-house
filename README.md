# Social House

**An experimental, reference-led social content operating system for small teams.**

Social House is being tested first with textile businesses, then designed to work across product and service sectors. A user should be able to add a few real product photos or clips, explain what they sell, and receive a publish-ready Instagram Reel or carousel—without being forced through an agency-sized briefing or an endless editing loop.

The standard is not “AI made a post.” The standard is: *would a real customer stop, understand the offer, trust it, and know what to do next?*

## What it is trying to solve

- Small businesses often have product material but not a creative director, editor, analyst, copywriter, or media buyer.
- Generic AI content follows a recognisable formula: invented claims, irrelevant model shots, loud text, repeated pacing, and no reason to message the business.
- Good-looking content alone does not create qualified enquiries. The offer, proof, CTA, response path, and learning loop must work together.

## The first two workflows

1. **Reel / short-form video** — a 9:16 product, process, story, or trend-adaptation Reel with cover, captions, audio plan, post copy, and a DM conversion path.
2. **Carousel** — a photo-led, legible, feed-tested carousel with a cover, slide narrative, post copy, alt text, and one clear action.

Both start with real assets. AI may help with support imagery, editing, copy, and motion—but it must never pretend that generated material is the actual product.

## Core principles

- **Brand before format.** A premium, playful, heritage, modern, or artisanal brand needs a coherent visual and verbal world before it needs a trend.
- **Proof before adjectives.** Show the weave, use, process, fit, material, result, or customer evidence. Do not merely say “premium.”
- **One memorable idea.** Every piece needs one thing a viewer can recall: a detail, line, movement, transformation, question, or reveal.
- **References are decomposed, never copied.** Learn their hook, crop, pace, sound role, and reveal mechanics; do not clone a creator’s work.
- **Quality gates over prompt faith.** Inspect layouts, safe zones, source truth, legibility, sound-off comprehension, and conversion before calling anything final.
- **Reach is not the goal by itself.** The desired outcome can be a DM, catalogue request, appointment, qualified lead, save, visit, or trust signal.

## What “one go” honestly means

The system should do its own internal creative passes, select one strongest direction, and return a finished delivery instead of making a new user micro-direct every decision.

It does **not** mean fabricating a beautiful answer from inadequate evidence. If the supplied material cannot prove the product, the output must say so and ask for the smallest useful missing shot—not disguise a draft as production-ready work.

## Current status

This repository is a **working specification and early skill**, not a promise of fully autonomous publishing, sales, or live trend access. The documented constraints are intentional.

We are actively looking for feedback from social media managers, founders, editors, photographers, designers, growth teams, and small businesses.

## Non-goals, for now

- Guaranteed reach, followers, enquiries, or sales.
- Automatic paid spend or publishing without an explicit, account-authorised approval step.
- Scraping or reusing music/content in ways that bypass platform rights or creator credit.
- Replacing professional shoots where a product needs material, fit, craft, or quality proof that no supplied asset contains.
